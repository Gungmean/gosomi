## Overall Impression

The welcome screen is reassuring and specific, and the form preview makes the outcome tangible. With the preview open, the chat column becomes narrow and messages wrap heavily. The single biggest opportunity is to give the conversation and the official form more balanced desktop space.

## What's Working

- The heading and mode descriptions use everyday Korean and reassure users who do not know legal terms or dates.
- The real police form preview makes the result concrete and keeps the draft beside the conversation.
- Privacy and review reminders appear near the point where users need them.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The side-by-side preview compresses the conversation.** At 1440×900, the open preview shares the workspace with the chat, causing messages and follow-up content to wrap heavily. This slows reading during a consequential task. **Fix:** let users resize the divider, or provide a wider-chat layout while keeping preview access obvious. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`.

2. **[P2] Secondary text is small and low contrast.** The overlay found 10 low-contrast text items and small text at 10–11px; examples include helper copy, sidebar metadata, and the composer placeholder. This makes supporting instructions harder to scan. **Fix:** raise contrast and set a more legible minimum size for decision-relevant helper text. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset`.

3. **[P2] Preview fields do not visibly signal that they can be edited.** The official form looks static, so first-time users may miss that values can be changed there. **Fix:** add a subtle edit affordance or a clear route from a field to “내용 수정.” **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.

4. **[P2] Recovery and contextual support are hard to find in the in-progress flow.** The inspected conversation screen does not make the next step after an AI request failure obvious. **Fix:** show a nearby retry/recovery action that preserves the user's draft, with concise contextual help. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`.
